About Wayfarer

Independent travel recommendations, curated by a small team.

Who we are

Wayfarer started as a shared document of places three friends kept recommending to each other. We turned it into a small site so anyone could browse beaches, temples, and countries worth visiting without wading through ads and sponsored posts.

Guidance for team members

If you're adding a new destination: confirm the name and country spelling, write a two-sentence description in plain language (no marketing fluff), and use a landscape-orientation image at least 800px wide. Tag every entry with exactly one category — beach, temple, or country — so search stays accurate. Open a pull request instead of committing directly to main.
